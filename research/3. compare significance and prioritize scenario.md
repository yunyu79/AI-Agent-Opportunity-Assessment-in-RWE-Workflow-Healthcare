# Pain Point Definitions

Definition 1 - **Manual processing burden:** Completing a task requires extensive manual operations, reading, or expert judgment, resulting in substantial human time investment.        

Definition 2 - **Repetitive work:** The same or similar information processing and operations are performed repeatedly.

Definition 3 - **Coordination and waiting:** Tasks depend on clarification, handoffs, confirmation, or approval across individuals or teams, creating additional communication effort and waiting time that extend delivery timelines.

Definition 4 - **Errors and rework:** Errors, omissions, or inconsistencies in data, logic, code, or outputs require additional checks, potentially including tracing back through the processes and results of multiple upstream steps.

Definition 5 - **Difficulty adapting and reusing existing work:** In new projects, existing logic, code, or deliverables cannot be readily adapted or reused, requiring substantial modification, adaptation, and revalidation.

- **W09:** Screen 1,000 titles/abstracts and extract approximately 75 articles. The batch effort estimates title screening and full-text review.

**Project-Defined Rating Scales**

These are project scoring conventions, not published industry standards. The assigned scores above are hypotheses requiring validation.

| Level | Task frequency, per team | Repetitive operation person-hours / total person-hours | Error rate: confirmed defective units / inspected units | Collaboration Time: active collaboration person-hours / total active person-hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Less than 1 task/month | Below 20% | Below 1% | Below 5% |
| 2 | 1–3 tasks/month | 20% to below 40% | 1% to below 3% | 5% to below 10% |
| 3 | 4–9 tasks/month | 40% to below 60% | 3% to below 5% | 10% to below 20% |
| 4 | 10–19 tasks/month | 60% to below 80% | 5% to below 10% | 20% to below 35% |
| 5 | At least 20 tasks/month | 80%–100% | 10%–100% | 35%–100% |

**Error rate:** Preserve the existing table entries as percentages, not ratings. For example, 5% maps to level 4, 3% to level 3, and 2% to level 2. Count each defective inspected unit once, even if it contains multiple defects. Define the inspected unit (e.g., screening decision, mapping, QC decision) before measurement and use the same denominator and review stage before and after AI. Disagreement is not a confirmed error without adjudication. Severity must be recorded separately; a low rate of critical errors can still be unacceptable.

**Collaboration Time:** This level measures the share of active labor spent on clarification, meetings, handoffs, confirmation and approvals. A one-hour meeting with three participants contributes three person-hours. Collaboration labor is part of total labor, not an additional cost to add again. Record elapsed approval/waiting days separately; calendar waiting is not convertible directly to person-hours. Existing collaboration levels remain unvalidated estimates until confirmed by time logs or structured interviews. These proposed thresholds are project conventions.

**Score calculation for each scenario**

- **Labor dimension:** $score = (manual_hours − AI-assisted_hours) / 320 × 100 * weight $. Calculating absolute time savings matters in the overall ranking Use 320 instead of each original hour to account for why 
- **Error dimension:** $$score = ({manual\_error\_percentage} − AI-assisted\_error\_percentage) / 5 × 100 * weight $$. 
- **Repetition dimension:** $score = ({manual_repetition_level} − AI_repetition_level) / 4 × 100 * weight $.
- **Frequency dimension:** $score = (baseline frequency level − 1) / 4 × 100 * weight$.
- **Collaboration dimension:** $score = (manual collaboration level − AI collaboration level) / 4 × 100 * weight$.
- **Overall score:** Sum of normalized weight × dimension score.
